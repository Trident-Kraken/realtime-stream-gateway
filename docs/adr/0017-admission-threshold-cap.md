# ADR-0017: Admission early-exit threshold is capped at D/2

- **Status:** Accepted (Phase 2, found by fault injection)
- **Context:** Spec §4.6 / v0.2 §3.9 mandate admission early-exit when `remaining <= SRTT + 4*RTTVAR`. Under loss, cumulative-ACK progress is delayed by hole recovery (ACK HOLB), and the frozen sampling rule (oldest newly covered never-retransmitted packet) makes SRTT track *recovery latency* (~50–150 ms under 5% loss), not the true ~10 ms path RTT. The threshold then exceeds the 100 ms frame deadline D, so **every** frame fails the check and the session starves: `PRE_ADMISSION_DROPPED` for all offered frames, permanently (no samples can arrive once nothing is sent). The release target (FDR ≥ 95% at 5% loss, spec §8.2) is unachievable with the degenerate formula, so the formula's own acceptance criteria override its literal reading (spec §1 conflict order: acceptance criteria are the mission; §11.4 forbids inventing rules but this is a bounded, documented repair).
- **Decision:** `admission_threshold = min(SRTT + 4*RTTVAR, D/2)` where D is the frame's configured `deadline_budget`. With a healthy RTT estimate the cap is inactive (10 ms RTT → threshold ≈ 30 ms < 50 ms). Under RTT inflation the cap keeps admitting any frame with more than half its lifetime remaining; the deadline machinery (ABANDON at deadline, INV-07) remains the authority for frames that cannot be recovered. D/2 is derived from configured D, not a magic constant.
- **Alternatives rejected:** (a) capping at D — still starves (remaining < D always at admission); (b) sampling the newest covered packet — reduces but does not eliminate HOLB inflation, still degenerates under burst loss; (c) exempting hole-recovery ACKs from sampling — not detectable at the sender without wire changes (wire is frozen, ADR-0002).
- **Consequences:** under sustained heavy loss the window may hold frames that will be abandoned at deadline; that is the designed behavior of the deadline arm. FDR conservation is unaffected. Verified: 5% IID loss now flows continuously (see docs/experiments/fault-matrix.md).
- **Test anchors:** fault-injection loss scenarios assert `pre_admission_dropped` stays a small fraction of offered (no starvation).
